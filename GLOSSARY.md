# Tell

Tell trains people to tell a real call from a scam by practicing on AI callers, and rewards good judgment with Blokbuks.

## Players and score

**Player**:
A person with a Tell profile, created by signing up with an email address.
_Avoid_: user, account, customer

**Blokbuks**:
Tell's points currency, shown as a plain "B" mark. Every Player starts at 0; correct play earns Blokbuks and wrong play loses them.
_Avoid_: points, money, coins, balance (the balance is the Challenge's fictional $1,000)

**Leaderboard**:
The national ranking of Players by Blokbuks within one Season.
_Avoid_: rankings, scoreboard

**Season**:
A fixed window of time with its own Leaderboard and prize list; the top three Players at its end can claim the prizes.
_Avoid_: challenge, contest, event

**Scored call**:
A call that earns or loses Blokbuks. A Player gets five per day; calls beyond that are practice and worth nothing.
_Avoid_: ranked call

**Scored moment**:
One point in a call where the Player's reply is judged, plus the Verdict at the end. Each one is either right or wrong.
_Avoid_: question, checkpoint

## Calls

**Call scenario**:
A playable practice call about one kind of call, such as a bank alert, a refund, an insurance check or a wrong number.
_Avoid_: theme, drill

**Version**:
The real or scam telling of a Call scenario, chosen at random each time it is played.
_Avoid_: variant, mode

**Category**:
The kind of call a Call scenario is about, such as bank fraud alert or holiday gift card. It never reveals the Call type.
_Avoid_: theme, topic, type

**Tactic**:
The pressure lever a caller leans on: Urgency, Authority, Details as proof, Reward or Fear.
_Avoid_: technique, trick

**Move**:
What one Player reply did: Probe, Protect, Verify, Disconnect, Leak or Comply.
_Avoid_: action, answer

**Call type**:
Who the caller really is: real, scam or hijacked. Hidden from the Player until after the Verdict.
_Avoid_: label, category

**Verdict**:
The Player's one-tap judgment after a call: Real, Scam or Unverified.
_Avoid_: answer, guess

**Challenge**:
The fixed, scripted day of three calls with a fictional $1,000 and two tasks.
_Avoid_: using it for the timed Leaderboard competition

## Analysis

**Player analysis**:
The personal read on what a Player commonly misses and does right, first shown after two completed calls and revised after every call after that.
_Avoid_: portfolio, report card, personalized dashboard

**Carrier insights**:
The anonymous dashboard for T-Mobile showing totals of which kinds of scam Players commonly fall for, by Category and Tactic.
_Avoid_: admin dashboard, T-Mobile info

## Agents

**Scorekeeper**:
The agent that labels each Player reply with a Move. It never decides Blokbuks.
_Avoid_: scorer, judge

**Player analyst**:
The agent that writes the Player analysis from one Player's call records.
_Avoid_: coach, profiler

**Insights analyst**:
The agent that writes the Carrier insights summary from anonymous totals only.
_Avoid_: admin agent, T-Mobile agent
